# Visual Identity & Asset Requirements
## The Soul Seed Design System

---

## VISUAL DESIGN DNA

### Style
- **Ethereal, iridescent, delicate, alive**
- Matte luminosity, translucent like fairy wings
- Silver sparkles (not stars)
- Breathing/alive quality

### Colors

**Primary Palette:**
- Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) - foundation
- Silver shimmer (`#E8E8F0`)
- Pale lavender (`#E6E6FA` at 40% opacity)
- Powder periwinkle (`#E0F4FF` at 30% opacity)
- Ghost white (`#F8F8FF`) with subtle blur

**Forbidden:**
- ❌ Black
- ❌ Pink
- ❌ Gold
- ❌ Glossy surfaces
- ❌ Bubble effects

### Feel
- Matte luminosity
- Translucent like fairy wings
- Silver sparkles (not stars)
- Breathing/alive
- Dust motes in moonlight

## TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

### General Specifications
- **Format:** PNG (high resolution)
- **Minimum width:** 1920px for slides
- **Backgrounds:** Consistent white (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Typography:** Thin, elegant fonts (SF Pro Display preferred)
- **Spacing:** Generous white space
- **Accents:** Subtle lavender/periwinkle only

---

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### The soul seed should NEVER look like:
- ❌ A glass bubble or sphere
- ❌ Plastic or glossy
- ❌ A traditional app icon
- ❌ A cartoon character

### It SHOULD look like:
- ✅ Translucent fairy wing membrane
- ✅ Vellum or rice paper with light behind it
- ✅ Dragonfly wing iridescence (subtle)
- ✅ Matte luminosity
- ✅ Organic, alive, breathing

### Sparkles should look like:
- ✅ Dust motes catching moonlight
- ✅ Delicate, ephemeral, drifting
- ❌ NOT stars or glitter
- ❌ NOT gold (only silver)

---

## ANIMATION SPECIFICATIONS

### Breathing Animation
```
Duration: 3 seconds
Scale: 1.0 → 1.03 → 1.0
Easing: ease-in-out
Loop: Infinite
```

### Sparkle Lifecycle
```
Spawn: Random around soul seed perimeter
Movement: Upward spiral (varies by state)
Fade: 3-5 seconds
Color: Silver with lavender/periwinkle edges
```

### States
- **Calm:** Lazy upward spirals, slow spawn rate
- **Excited:** Faster swirls, tighter orbits, increased spawn
- **Passionate:** Whirling effect, tight spirals, concentrated energy

---

## BRAND VOICE

### Typography Treatment
- **Font:** SF Pro Display Light
- **Sizes:** 18-22pt for whisper text
- **Letter-spacing:** 0.5-1pt (wide)
- **Line height:** 1.5x (generous)
- **Color:** White on semi-transparent backgrounds
- **Style:** Thin, spaced, breathable

### Tone Principles
- Short sentences
- Frequent pauses (line breaks)
- Breathy, poetic phrasing
- Never robotic or transactional

## USAGE NOTES

### For Developers
- Use asset catalogs in Xcode
- @2x and @3x variants for all assets
- SVG preferred for scalable elements
- Metal shaders for particle effects

### For Designers
- Maintain consistent color profiles
- Export at 2x resolution minimum
- Preserve transparency where applicable
- Test on actual Apple Watch displays

### For Presentations
- Use PNG for slides (not PDF)
- Ensure legibility at projection sizes
- Maintain aspect ratios
- Keep file sizes reasonable (<5MB per image)
