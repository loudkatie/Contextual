# Product Vision — Contextual

Contextual is the first **ambient, audio-first assistant** designed to quietly support people as they
move through the world. Not another chatbot. Not another feed. A contextual layer that awakens when
the environment, moment, and user align.

The product answers a simple question:

**“What is the one helpful thing this person would want to know *right now* in this place?"**

Contextual uses signals from geolocation, device motion, calendar intent, and a personal profile to
deliver *light, timely whispers* through AirPods. It is proactive but never interruptive. Helpful but
never heavy. Invisible until needed.

---

## Why this matters now

Three converging shifts make Contextual possible:

### 1. **AirPods are becoming a computing platform**  
People wear them for hours per day, across transit, errands, and work sessions. This opens new UX
terrain where the screen is optional.

### 2. **LLMs enable intent inference**  
Personal context can now be interpreted in real time:
- where the user is,
- what they’re doing,
- what they care about,
- what they want to avoid,
- what they might love.

### 3. **Location is a new frontier for AI**  
Most assistants today respond to prompts. Contextual responds to *place*.  
Geo-triggered intelligence is the next AI interface.

---

## Product principles

These rules guide every feature:

### **1. Feel magical, not mechanical**  
Proactive notifications should arrive only when they feel like luck, intuition, or serendipity.

### **2. Whisper, don’t shout**  
Short audio nudges. No long explanations. No multiturn overwhelm. The app is there to support, not
dominate.

### **3. Always earn the interruption**  
If a whisper isn’t valuable in that exact moment, it shouldn’t fire.

### **4. Build trust through accuracy**  
The system should get noticeably smarter the more someone uses it — but without ever feeling
surveillant.

### **5. Be invisible when not needed**  
The best version of Contextual is the one the user forgets is running.

---

## Core user value

Users get:

- *Timely prompts* when crossing relevant geogates  
- *Reminders aligned with intention and moment*  
- *Discovery that feels personalized and surprising*  
- *A sense of quiet support throughout the day*  

Contextual is not a to-do app or navigation tool.  
It’s a **lens** that helps people notice what matters.

---

## North Star Experience

1. User puts in AirPods.
2. Walks through the city.
3. As they enter a meaningful location (restaurant they like, store with an expiring benefit, a place
   tied to personal history, or an opportunity aligned with their goals)…
4. A soft whisper offers:
   **“Want the table that just opened up?”**  
   **“Your prescription discount expires today.”**  
   **“There’s a friend nearby.”**  
   **“A memory happened right here.”**

The world feels *alive* with relevance.

## Roadmap (high-level)

### **Phase 1 — Foundations**
- iOS app + AirPods audio output
- Simple geogates
- Whisper engine (manual rules + LLM refinement)
- Personalization basics (home, work, favorites)

### **Phase 2 — Intelligence**
- Intent inference model
- Time-of-day behavioral patterns
- Personal profile builder
- Opportunity ranking model

### **Phase 3 — Distributed experience**
- Partner APIs (restaurants, commerce, events)
- Travel mode
- Memory mode (Kilroy integration)
- Social proximity signals

### **Phase 4 — Always-on proactive ecosystem**
- Third-party developer surfaces
- Business graph  
- AI-curated city layers  
- Predictive embeddings for life patterns
