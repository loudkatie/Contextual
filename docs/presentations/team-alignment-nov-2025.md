## SLIDE 4: THREE CONTEXTS, ONE SOUL SEED

**"She lives in three moments of your life."**

### CONTEXT 1: WALKING (Memories)
*The Kilroy evolution*

- Geo-tied photos, videos, messages
- But **proactive**: she whispers when you enter locations with content
- Privacy circles (who sees your memories)
- **Example:** *"Katie left a memory here last spring. Want to see?"*

### CONTEXT 2: WATCHING (TV/Sports)
*The big new capability*

- Audio fingerprinting syncs with what's on screen
- Whispers insider facts during natural pauses
- Works with ANY content (NBC, ESPN, Netflix)
- **Example:** *"Rainn Wilson actually started that fire without telling the cast."*

### CONTEXT 3: SHOPPING (Perks)
*Future phase*

- Loyalty programs, expiring benefits
- Partner APIs (Sephora, airlines, stores)
- **Example:** *"You're VIB at Sephora. 40% off today only."*
